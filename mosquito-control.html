Mosquito Control: Enjoy Your Yard Again

Mosquitoes don't just ruin backyard evenings — they carry diseases that put your family and pets at risk. Professional mosquito control dramatically reduces the population so you can actually use your outdoor space.

Bug Bouncers connects you with a licensed pro who treats your yard at the source and keeps mosquitoes away through the season.

Where Mosquitoes Breed

  • Standing water in gutters, planters, and toys
  • Shaded, humid areas under decks and shrubs
  • Birdbaths, ponds, and clogged drains
  • Tall grass and dense landscaping

How Professional Treatment Works

A technician treats resting and breeding areas and can install automatic misting systems for continuous protection during peak season.

  • Barrier treatments on resting areas
  • Larvicide for standing-water breeding sites
  • Automatic misting system options
  • Recurring seasonal service plans

Seasonal Protection

Mosquito pressure peaks in warm months. Recurring treatments keep your yard usable all season instead of just after a single spray.
