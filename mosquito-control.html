Where Mosquitoes Breed

  • Standing water in gutters, planters, and toys
  • Shaded, humid areas under decks and shrubs
  • Birdbaths, ponds, and clogged drains
  • Tall grass and dense landscaping

How Professional Treatment Works

A technician treats resting and breeding areas and can install automatic misting systems for continuous protection during peak season.
