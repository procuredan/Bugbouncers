Spider Control: Clear The Webs And Keep Them Gone

Most spiders are harmless, but a few — like black widows and brown recluses — pose real risks, and nobody wants webs in every corner. Professional spider control reduces the population and the insects that draw spiders in.

Bug Bouncers matches you with a licensed pro who treats spiders and the pests they feed on.

When To Worry About Spiders

  • Black widow spiders in garages and woodpiles
  • Brown recluse spiders in closets and storage
  • Heavy webbing around eaves and corners
  • Egg sacs that signal a growing population

How Pros Handle Spiders

Spiders come for food, so effective control treats both the spiders and the insects they hunt.

  • De-webbing of eaves, corners, and entryways
  • Targeted treatment of harborage areas
  • Reducing the insect prey that attracts spiders
  • Exterior barrier treatments

Safety First

If you suspect venomous spiders, don't handle them yourself. A licensed technician can identify and treat the problem safely.
