Termite Control: Protect Your Home From Costly Damage

Termites cause billions of dollars in property damage every year, and most homeowners never see them until the damage is done. Routine inspections and professional treatment are the best protection for one of your largest investments.

Bug Bouncers connects you with a licensed termite specialist who can inspect, treat, and set up ongoing protection for your property.

Warning Signs Of Termites

  • Mud tubes along foundations and walls
  • Hollow-sounding or sagging wood
  • Discarded wings near windows and doors
  • Bubbling or cracked paint that looks like water damage

Termite Treatment Options

The right treatment depends on the species and the structure. A licensed pro will recommend the most effective option and often provide a renewable warranty.

  • Liquid soil treatments that create a protective barrier
  • In-ground bait stations that eliminate the colony
  • Wood treatments and spot treatments for active areas
  • Annual inspections and renewable protection plans

Prevention Pays Off

Ongoing monitoring is far cheaper than structural repairs. A yearly inspection catches problems early and keeps your warranty active.
