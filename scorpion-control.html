If you live in the desert Southwest, scorpions aren't just a nuisance — the Arizona bark scorpion delivers the most painful and medically significant sting in the U.S., and it's most active from spring through fall, surging in the summer heat and during monsoon season. Store-bought sprays barely dent the problem because scorpions hide in tiny cracks, hunt at night, and even glow under UV light. Professional scorpion control is the only reliable way to take back your home.

Why Scorpions Are So Hard to Control

  • They squeeze through gaps as thin as a credit card
  • They're nocturnal and hide in walls, block fences, and garages by day
  • They're hunting the crickets, roaches, and spiders already around your home
  • Monsoon storms flush them out of the desert and toward houses

How Professional Scorpion Treatment Works

A licensed scorpion specialist attacks the problem on multiple fronts — killing the scorpions, removing what they feed on, and sealing the ways they get inside.

  • Exterior barrier treatments around the foundation, block walls, and entry points
  • Sealing gaps, weep holes, garage thresholds, and other entry routes
  • Knocking down the insect prey scorpions hunt
  • UV black-light inspections to find harborage
  • Recurring service to break the seasonal cycle

Stay Ahead of Scorpion Season

Scorpion pressure builds fast once nighttime temperatures climb. A one-time treatment helps, but recurring service through the warm months is what keeps a scorpion-free home — and gives families with kids and pets real peace of mind.
