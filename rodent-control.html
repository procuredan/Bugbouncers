Rodent Control: Get Rid Of Mice And Rats For Good

Mice and rats do more than raise the ick factor — they chew wiring, contaminate food, and spread disease. Traps from the store might catch a few, but lasting control means removing the rodents and sealing the way they got in.

Bug Bouncers matches you with a licensed pro who handles the full job: removal, exclusion, and clean-up.

Signs Of A Rodent Problem

  • Droppings in cabinets, drawers, or along walls
  • Gnaw marks on food packaging, wires, or wood
  • Scratching or scurrying sounds in walls and ceilings
  • Nests made of shredded paper or insulation

How Pros Stop Rodents

Effective rodent control is a system, not a single trap. A licensed technician removes the active population, then seals entry points so new rodents can't return.

  • Inspection to find entry points and nesting areas
  • Trapping and removal of the active population
  • Exclusion work to seal gaps, vents, and openings
  • Sanitation and prevention guidance

Why Exclusion Matters

Rodents can squeeze through a hole the size of a dime. Without sealing entry points, the problem comes right back — which is why professional exclusion is the key to lasting results.
