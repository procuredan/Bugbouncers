Pigeon & Bird Control: Clear the Birds and the Mess They Leave

Pigeons and other nuisance birds do more than make noise. Their droppings carry disease, corrode rooftops and equipment, clog solar panels and gutters, and create slip hazards and ugly stains at storefronts. Once a flock claims a roost, they're stubborn — professional bird control is what actually moves them along and keeps them gone.

Bug Bouncers matches you with a licensed local pro for humane, effective pigeon and bird control at homes and businesses.

Where Birds Become a Problem

  • Solar panels — pigeons nest in the gap underneath
  • Roof lines, ledges, signs, and rafters
  • Patios, balconies, and entryways with droppings
  • Warehouses, storefronts, and loading docks

How Professional Bird Control Works

A licensed pro picks the right mix of exclusion and deterrents for your property, then cleans and sanitizes the mess the birds left behind.

  • Netting to block roosting in patios, eaves, and warehouses
  • Spikes, wire, and slope deterrents on ledges and signs
  • Solar panel exclusion mesh
  • Nest and droppings removal with sanitizing
  • Ongoing deterrence so they don't resettle

Protect Your Property and Health

Bird droppings aren't just unsightly — they're a health and liability concern, and they degrade roofs and equipment over time. Fast, professional bird control protects your property, your customers, and your bottom line.
