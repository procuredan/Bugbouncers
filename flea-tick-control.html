Flea And Tick Control: Protect Your Home, Yard, And Pets

Fleas and ticks aren't just a pet problem — they infest carpets, bedding, and yards, and ticks can spread serious illnesses like Lyme disease. Professional treatment breaks the life cycle indoors and out.

Bug Bouncers matches you with a licensed pro who treats your home and yard together for lasting relief.

Signs Of Fleas And Ticks

  • Pets scratching, biting, or losing fur
  • Flea dirt (tiny black specks) in pet bedding
  • Bites around ankles and lower legs
  • Ticks found on pets or family after time outdoors

How Professional Treatment Works

A technician treats both the indoor environment and the yard, targeting eggs and larvae as well as adults to stop the cycle.

  • Interior treatment of carpets, bedding, and cracks
  • Yard treatment of shaded, humid harborage areas
  • Growth regulators to stop reproduction
  • Coordination with your pet's vet treatment

Why Treat The Whole Cycle

Most of a flea population is eggs and larvae you can't see. Treating only adults leaves the infestation to rebound — professional treatment targets every stage.
